Mechanism: an exact, chain-invariant tie in S0
==============================================================================
S0 is chain-invariant and ties EXACTLY (S0=12.151582) between ['(0, 1, 9)', '(0, 8, 9)'] at every one of the 12 held-out chains (and identity) -- both
triples share anchor orbitals {0,9}, differing only in the third (1 vs 8); their
Jab diagonal entries at orbitals 1 and 8 are numerically identical for this system.
The tie is broken deterministically in favour of (0,1,9) by lexicographic ordering
(itertools.combinations lists it before (0,8,9)) -- not by anything informative about
which is the better choice at a given chain.

CHARACTERISATION
==============================================================================
newchain11 is a single anomalous chain, not a sign of systematic S0 failure. Its
regret_frac>1.0 has an identified, structural cause: S0 has a genuine, chain-invariant
EXACT tie between two real anchor choices that differ by ~47-51 mHa in true outcome,
broken by an uninformative lexicographic rule; the lexicographic pick is the worse of
the two at 6/13 chains where both were evaluated (identity + the 12
held-out chains), yet regret_frac exceeds 1.0 ONLY at newchain11 -- because that chain
independently has the smallest error range and lowest floor of the set, shrinking the
normalisation denominator (44.06 mHa) far more than the tie's absolute cost shrinks.
S0's correlation with captured at newchain11 (+0.609, correct sign, p<0.05) is unaffected
by this -- the tie only degrades the single top PICK, not the score's overall ranking
quality.
